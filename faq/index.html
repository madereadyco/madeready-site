What does Made Ready do?

I build websites for small UK trades, starting with plumbers and heating engineers. I build a free demo of your new site first. If you like it, I launch it on your own hosting and domain, then look after it for a monthly fee.

Do I need to already have a website?

No. A weak, old or Wix site is fine. A fresh Made Ready site is built for you and your content is moved across. No site at all is fine too.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it first, you look at it, and you pay nothing if you say no. Demos are not public and are deleted within 30 days if you don't go ahead.

How much does a website cost?

A one-page site is from £350 and a site of up to 5 pages is from £750. Extra work is £35 an hour. I quote the exact price after you've seen your demo. See the full cost breakdown.

How much is a care plan?

Care plans are £45 (Essential), £75 (Standard) or £120 (Premium) a month. See what each one covers.

What is a care plan?

A monthly plan where I keep your WordPress site updated, secure and backed up, watch for downtime, and send you a monthly report. Standard and Premium also look after your Google Maps listing and include minutes of edits each month. Hosting is not included.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with Standard. It includes your Google Maps listing and 30 minutes of edits a month. Choose Essential if you'll never need changes or Google help, and Premium if you want edits and answers fast.

Who owns my domain and hosting?

You do. You sign up with the host and pay them directly, and I'll help you choose and set it up. I never host your live site and I'm not your hosting provider. That means you can walk away at any time and keep everything.

How much does hosting cost?

Usually £5 to £15 a month, paid directly to your host. It isn't part of my care plan.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, your services, the areas you cover, and a few photos of your work. I can use what is on your current site and tighten it.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, simple text and photo changes are easy and I show you how. Or send them to me. Standard and Premium plans include minutes of edits each month. Email me your requests and I do them in a weekly batch. On Essential, edits are charged hourly.

Will I show up in Google Maps?

Standard and Premium plans include setting up and looking after your Google Business Profile, the listing people see in Google Maps. I add your services, area, hours and photos, help you ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to them for you. You stay the owner and add me as a manager. I can't promise a ranking, and I never write or buy reviews. They have to come from your real customers.

How do I cancel?

Email me and say you want to stop. Care plans roll monthly, so you give 30 days' notice and I stop billing at the end of that notice. There is no cancellation fee. You keep your domain, your hosting and your site. See the full terms.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Care plans roll monthly and you give 30 days' notice to stop. I'll put the terms in writing before you pay anything.

Where are you based?

Leicester. I work with small trades across the UK, and everything can be done online.

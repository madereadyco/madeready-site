Made Ready Budget Tracker

A Google Sheets budget tracker built specifically for UK bank accounts.

Import CSV statements from Monzo, Chase, Starling, Barclays, and Revolut.

Auto-categorise spending, track net worth, and see a live safe-to-spend figure —

all inside your own Google account. No subscription. One-time purchase.

Personal productivity

Live

Made Ready 12-Week Planner

A Google Sheets system that automates the 12 Week Year methodology — treating

twelve weeks with the urgency of a full year. Set up to three goals, plan weekly

tactics, and let the sheet auto-calculate your execution score and habit tracking.

Pro adds an AI coach that reviews your scorecard and flags goals going off track

before the cycle ends. No subscription. One-time purchase, reusable every cycle.

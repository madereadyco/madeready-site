What we do

Beyond the products

Alongside our own products, we take on a small number of well-scoped projects with founders and teams who need thoughtful frontend work with a strong design sensibility.

  • React design systems Component libraries, token systems, and UI kits for product teams who want to move faster without losing quality.

The studio

A design education.
A builder's mindset.

Made Ready Co is run by Rol — a developer and designer based in Leicester, UK, with a background in fine art and print design from Central Saint Martins in London. Seven years building things for the web. A lifetime caring about how things look and feel.

The combination isn't common. Most developers don't art-direct. Most designers don't build. The studio exists in that gap — making products that work properly and look like someone genuinely thought about them.

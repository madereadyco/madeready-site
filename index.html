How your plan works

Google Maps and reviews (Standard and Premium). When someone nearby searches for what you do, your business should show up in Google Maps with reviews that make them call you. I set up your Google Business Profile with your services, area, hours, photos and website link, and keep it up to date. You stay the owner and add me as a manager. Google will ask you to confirm it's your business, and only you can do that. Each month I post photos of your work, draft replies to new reviews and send a short report of calls, direction requests and website clicks. I give you a review link, a QR code and a text to send customers. You ask them, because I can't do that for you, and I never write or buy reviews. I can't promise where you'll rank. If someone else already controls your listing, getting access can take a while.

Email me your edit requests. I do them in a weekly batch, so there's no phone support. Unused edit minutes don't carry over to the next month. Anything outside your plan, like new pages or features, I'll quote first, so there are no surprises. Hosting isn't included: you pay your host directly and you own it. It's rolling monthly, with 30 days' notice to cancel, and no VAT to add. Read the full terms.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to already have a website?

No. A weak, old or Wix site is fine. A fresh Made Ready site is built for you and your content is moved across. No site at all is fine too.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it first, you look at it, and you pay nothing if you say no. Demos are not public and are deleted within 30 days if you don't go ahead.

Who owns my domain and hosting?

You do. You sign up with the host and pay them directly. I never host your live site. That means you can walk away at any time and keep everything.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, your services, the areas you cover, and a few photos of your work. I can use what is on your current site and tighten it.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, simple text and photo changes are easy and I show you how. Or send them to me. Standard and Premium plans include minutes of edits each month. They don't roll over. Email me your requests and I do them in a weekly batch. On Essential, edits are charged hourly.

Will I show up in Google Maps?

Standard and Premium plans include setting up and looking after your Google Business Profile, the listing people see in Google Maps. I add your services, area, hours and photos, help you ask happy customers for reviews, and reply to them for you. You stay the owner and add me as a manager. I can't promise a ranking, and I never write or buy reviews. They have to come from your real customers.

What is a care plan?

A monthly plan where I keep your WordPress site updated, secure and backed up, watch for downtime, and send you a monthly report. Standard and Premium also look after your Google Maps listing and include minutes of edits each month. Hosting is not included because you pay your host directly.

How do I cancel?

Email me and tell me you want to stop. Care plans roll monthly, so you give 30 days' notice and I stop billing at the end of that notice. There is no cancellation fee. You keep your domain, your hosting and your site. See the full terms.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Care plans roll monthly and you give 30 days notice to stop. I'll put the terms in writing before you pay anything.

What does it cost?

Care plans are £45, £75 or £120 a month. The redesign itself is quoted after you've seen your demo, so you decide with the real thing in front of you.

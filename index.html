Why it matters

Your reviews get them to your website. Then what?

Someone reads your reviews and likes what they see. Then they tap your website. If it's slow, dated or fiddly on a phone, some of them stop there.

Most online time is on a phone

UK adults spend about four and a half hours online a day, and most of that time is on a smartphone. Your site has to work there first.

People look for proof first

Before buying from a business they don't know, 71% of British adults check reviews and 45% look for clear contact details. Both belong on your site.

Sources: Ofcom, "From apps to AI search: how the UK goes online in 2025", 10 December 2025. YouGov, 2,119 GB adults, December 2025 (shoppers considering unfamiliar online businesses).

Rent or own

A directory listing is rented. This site is yours.

Directories can send you calls. If yours pays for itself, keep it. But it's a rented shop window: you pay every month, and when you stop paying, your profile and reviews stay behind. Checkatrade's basic plan, for example, starts from £30 + VAT a month.

A directory listing

  • A monthly fee, for as long as you want to be listed
  • Your profile and reviews stay with them if you leave
  • Your competitors are listed right beside you

Your Made Ready site

  • Your domain, your hosting, your content
  • Stop with 30 days' notice and it all stays online
  • Your Google reviews are yours, and they stay with the business

Use both if you like. Just make sure one of them is yours. Source: Checkatrade, "Does Checkatrade cost money?", last updated 19 December 2024. Other directories price differently.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to already have a website?

No. A weak, old or Wix site is fine. A fresh Made Ready site is built for you and your content is moved across. No site at all is fine too.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it first, you look at it, and you pay nothing if you say no. Demos are not public and are deleted within 30 days if you don't go ahead.

Who owns my domain and hosting?

You do. You sign up with the host and pay them directly, and I'll help you choose and set it up. I never host your live site and I'm not your hosting provider. That means you can walk away at any time and keep everything.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, your services, the areas you cover, and a few photos of your work. I can use what is on your current site and tighten it.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, simple text and photo changes are easy and I show you how. Or send them to me. Standard and Premium plans include minutes of edits each month. Email me your requests and I do them in a weekly batch. On Essential, edits are charged hourly.

Will I show up in Google Maps?

Standard and Premium plans include setting up and looking after your Google Business Profile, the listing people see in Google Maps. You stay the owner. I can't promise a ranking, and I never write or buy reviews. They come from your real customers. The details are under care plans.

What is a care plan?

A monthly plan where I keep your WordPress site updated, secure and backed up, watch for downtime, and send you a monthly report. Standard and Premium also look after your Google Maps listing and include minutes of edits each month. Hosting is not included. You pay your host directly and I'll help you set it up. Uptime alerts tell you if your site goes down, but keeping the server running is your host's job.

How do I cancel?

Email me and tell me you want to stop. Care plans roll monthly, so you give 30 days' notice and I stop billing at the end of that notice. There is no cancellation fee. You keep your domain, your hosting and your site. See the full terms.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Care plans roll monthly and you give 30 days notice to stop. I'll put the terms in writing before you pay anything.

What does it cost?

Care plans are £45, £75 or £120 a month. A one-page site is from £350 and a site of up to 5 pages is from £750. I quote the exact price after you've seen your demo, so you decide with the real thing in front of you.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with Standard. It includes your Google Maps listing and 30 minutes of edits a month. Choose Essential if you'll never need changes or Google help, and Premium if you want edits and answers fast.

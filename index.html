Personal productivity

Live

Made Ready 12-Week Planner

A Google Sheets system that automates the 12 Week Year methodology — treating twelve weeks with the urgency of a full year. Set up to three goals, plan weekly tactics, and let the sheet auto-calculate your execution score and habit tracking. Pro adds an AI coach that reviews your scorecard and flags goals going off track before the cycle ends. No subscription. One-time purchase, reusable every cycle.

Privacy notice

Last updated 22 November 2026. This explains what personal information I collect through madeready.co and when I contact businesses, why, and what your rights are. I follow UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Who I am

Made Ready is run by Roland Levy in Leicester, UK, and I am responsible for your information. Contact me at madeready.co (at) gmail.com.

What I collect

If you use the contact form, I get your name, business name, email address, website, the care plan you picked if you clicked a plan button, and any message you write. If you email me instead, I get what you send.

This site has no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. It is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested, to run and secure the service.

The contact form

The form is handled by Web3Forms, a third-party service. It receives what you type and passes it to my email inbox. Its own privacy policy explains how it handles data.

Why I use it, and the legal basis

I use your details to reply to you, to build the free demo you ask for, and to provide and bill for any work you go ahead with. This is needed to take steps at your request before a contract, and to carry out a contract with you. I also have a legitimate interest in answering enquiries and keeping simple records of them.

How long I keep it

If you ask for a demo and don't go ahead, the demo is deleted within 30 days and I keep your enquiry email for up to 12 months in case you get back in touch, then delete it. If you become a client, I keep your records for as long as the work continues and then for as long as the law requires for business and tax records.

Who I share it with

I don't sell your information. It passes through the services I use to run Made Ready: Web3Forms (the contact form), my email provider, and Vercel (hosting). If you become a client I may also use an invoicing or payment provider. I share it with others only if the law requires it.

Business outreach

I may contact businesses I think I can help. I find their details from public sources such as Companies House, Google listings and the business's own website. I only email limited companies and LLPs, using a business email address. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships I contact by phone (checked against the Telephone Preference Service), by letter or in person. Every message says who I am and why I'm writing. If you don't want to hear from me, tell me and I'll stop and keep only your name or business name on a do-not-contact list so I don't contact you again. The legal basis is legitimate interests.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, restrict how I use it, or object to me using it, including for marketing. Email me and I'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. I'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

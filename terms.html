These are the terms for a Made Ready care plan. In plain English, so you know where you stand.

What a care plan covers

Every plan includes WordPress and plugin updates, security checks, backup monitoring, uptime alerts and a monthly report. Standard and Premium also include minutes of edits each month, and Google Business Profile and review help (see below). Essential includes no edits and no Google profile work.

Google Business Profile and reviews (Standard and Premium)

  • I set up your Google Business Profile once, with your services, area, hours, photos and website link, then keep it up to date. This is included in the plan price.
  • You own the profile. You add me as a manager and can remove me at any time. Google's check that the business is yours has to be done by you.
  • Each month I post photos or updates, draft replies to new reviews and send a short report of calls, direction requests and website clicks, taken from Google's own figures.
  • I give you a review link, a QR code and wording to send customers. You ask your customers for reviews. I never write, buy or edit reviews, and I won't ask you to. Reviews must come from real customers.
  • I can't promise rankings, calls or any number of reviews. Google decides what it shows.
  • If someone else already controls your profile, or Google takes a long time to verify it, setup can take longer. I'll tell you where it stands.
  • Replies I draft are posted in your name once you're happy with them, or by me if you've told me to.

Prices

Essential is £45 a month, Standard £75 and Premium £120. No VAT is added. I'll tell you in writing before any price changes, and you can cancel before it does.

Edit requests

  • Send requests by email only. There is no phone support for edits.
  • I do edits in a weekly batch, not the moment they arrive.
  • Standard includes 30 minutes of edits a month. Premium includes 90 minutes.
  • Unused minutes don't carry over to the next month.
  • On Essential, or once your minutes are used, extra edits are charged hourly. I'll tell you the cost before I start.

Reply times

Essential: within 5 working days. Standard: within 3 working days. Premium: a priority reply within 2 working days. A reply means I've seen your message and told you what happens next. It doesn't always mean the work is finished.

Work outside your plan

New pages, new features and anything else outside your plan are quoted first. I don't start until you've said yes to the price.

Hosting and ownership

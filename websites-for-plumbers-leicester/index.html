Questions

Plumbers ask me this.

Do you only work with plumbers in Leicester?

I'm based in Leicester and I'm starting with plumbers and heating engineers, but I build sites for small trades anywhere in the UK. Everything is done online, so there's no need to meet.

Do I need a new website if I'm already on Checkatrade or MyBuilder?

Directory profiles can send you work, so keep them if they pay for themselves. But the profile belongs to the directory. A website on your own domain stays yours, and it's the one place you control what customers see. Plenty of tradespeople use both.

Can you show my Gas Safe number and other badges?

Yes, if you hold them. I only show registrations and memberships you actually have, and I'll ask to see them first. Customers like to check, so they go where they're easy to find.

Will I show up first on Google?

I can't promise that, and nobody honest can. What I can do is build a clear, fast site, make sure your services and areas are written in plain words, and (on Standard and Premium) look after your Google Business Profile. Reviews have to come from your real customers.

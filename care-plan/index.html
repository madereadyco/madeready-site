What a website care plan covers

A care plan is a monthly fee for someone to look after your website, so you can get on with the job. Mine cost £45, £75 or £120 a month. Here's exactly what you get, and what you don't.

Why bother

Sites break when nobody looks after them.

A WordPress site needs regular updates to stay secure and working. Plugins go out of date. Backups fail without anyone noticing. If you're up a ladder, you won't spot any of it. A care plan means someone does.

In plain English

What each part means.

Updates and security checks

I keep WordPress itself and your plugins up to date, and check for known security problems. Out-of-date software is a common way sites get broken into.

Backup monitoring and uptime alerts

I check your backups are actually running, so there's something to restore if the worst happens. Uptime alerts tell me if your site goes down. Keeping the server running is your host's job, so I let you both know.

The monthly report

A short email each month listing what was updated, that backups ran, and how the site stayed up.

Edits

Email me what you want changed and I do it in a weekly batch. That's new opening hours, a price change or a swapped photo. There's no phone support, which keeps the price down. On Essential, edits are charged hourly.

Google Maps and reviews (Standard and Premium)

I set up your Google Business Profile with your services, area, hours, photos and website link, and keep it current. You stay the owner and add me as a manager. Google asks you to confirm it's your business, and only you can do that. Each month I post photos of your work, draft replies to new reviews and send a short report of calls, direction requests and website clicks. I give you a review link, a QR code and a text to send customers. You ask them. I never write or buy reviews, and I can't promise where you'll rank.

The limits

What a care plan doesn't cover.

Hosting and your domain

You own both and pay your provider directly. I'll help you set them up. I don't provide hosting.

New pages and features

Anything outside your plan is quoted before I start, at £35 an hour.

Questions

Care plan questions.

What is a website care plan?

A monthly plan where I keep your WordPress site updated, secure and backed up, watch for downtime and send you a monthly report. Standard and Premium also look after your Google Maps listing and include minutes of edits each month.

How much does a care plan cost?

Essential is £45 a month, Standard is £75 and Premium is £120. Hosting is not included. You pay your host directly, usually £5 to £15 a month.

Is hosting included in a care plan?

No. You own your hosting account and pay the host directly. I'll help you choose and set it up, but I'm not your hosting provider, and keeping the server online is your host's job.

Can I cancel a care plan?

Yes. Plans roll monthly. Email me, give 30 days' notice and I stop billing at the end of it. There's no cancellation fee, and you keep your domain, hosting and site.

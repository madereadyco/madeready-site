Before you hire anyone

Five questions to ask any web designer.

Who owns the domain and hosting?

It should be you. With me it is, and you can walk away and keep everything.

Can I see it before I pay?

With me, yes. The demo is free and private.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Ask for a list. Mine is on the care plan page.

What does it cost to leave?

With me, nothing. 30 days' notice and no cancellation fee.

Is there a minimum term?

Not with me. Plans roll monthly.

Three things: my care plan (£45, £75 or £120 a month), your hosting (usually £5 to £15 a month, paid to your host) and your domain name (paid to your registrar once a year).

No. The demo is free. You only pay if you like it and want it live.

Is there a long contract?

No. Care plans roll monthly and you give 30 days' notice to stop. I'll put the terms in writing before you pay anything.

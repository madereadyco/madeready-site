Pell & Wren: 10 icon ideas

Icon only. Type is on hold. Concept drawings in chunky flanged pipework, teal and amber. Each is shown large, at 32px, in one colour and on mint.

My top 3

10. Drop from a pipe bend (my pick)

Best standalone icon. One shape, instantly water plus pipework, the union nuts make it plumbing and not a generic droplet, and it holds up at 32px and in one colour. Weakness: drop logos are everywhere, so it is clean rather than surprising.

1. Pipe ampersand (runner-up, the clever one)

Ties to the name "pell & wren" and nobody else has it. Reads as an ampersand and as pipework. Weakness: at 32px the collars and drip turn to texture, so it needs the larger size or a simplified small version.

2. Pipe bend into a P (third)

Clear P, clearly pipe, very readable small. Weakness: the safe choice, and it is only the initial, with no wit beyond the drop in its eye.

Not in my top 3: 3 reads as UU or WW at small sizes. 4, 5 and 8 are good icons but look like stock plumbing symbols. 6 is bold but says "T". 7 reads as a donut or a button. 9 is pretty but says "knot" or "Celtic", not plumber.

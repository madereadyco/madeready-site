Pell & Wren: logo options

Concept only. Plumbing ideas only, no box. The wordmark is outlined (Inter ExtraBold, open licence), so it looks the same everywhere. Recommended: A.

A. Pipe bends into a P (recommended)

A pipe run that bends into a P, with a union nut on the stem and a drop in the eye of the P.

Icon
32px
One colour
On mint

Honest take: the strongest. It is clearly a pipe, clearly a P, and the drop in the P’s eye is the clever bit. It reads at 32px and in one colour. Weakness: a big P above a lowercase "pell" repeats the initial, and the amber union nut is the first detail to blur when small.

B. Cross-head tap

Honest take: instantly says tap, and it is simple and bold. But it is a generic icon you could find in any stock set, and it carries no letter or idea of its own. Safe, not distinctive.
